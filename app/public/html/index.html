You My groundnut leaves are turning yellow. What should I do?
Oxian Yellowing in groundnut can indicate iron deficiency (common in alkaline patches of red soil) or early leaf spot disease. Since your soil is red and rain-fed, I'd recommend checking for circular brown spots on lower leaves. If spots are present, apply Mancozeb at 2.5g/L. For iron deficiency, a foliar spray of 0.5% FeSO₄ works well.
